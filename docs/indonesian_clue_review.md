## Sense and ambiguity review

- COLUMN: newspaper opinion feature; PILLAR: load-bearing upright.
- CHOICE: selection act; OPTION: one available alternative.
- CITE: cite a source as evidence; QUOTE: reproduce words exactly.
- HINDER: impede task progress; IMPEDE: obstacles slowing physical movement.
- AMBLE: slow relaxed steps; SAUNTER: stroll to enjoy surroundings.
- ENGINE: fuel combustion/pistons; MOTOR: electricity converted to rotation.
- RAVEN/CROW: large wedge tail vs common fan tail.
- INFER/DEDUCE: supplied evidence vs general principles.
- BLUNT/TERSE/CURT: unsoftened speech vs cold brevity vs rude abruptness.
- LIGHT remains illumination; WATCH a wrist timepiece; NEST an arranging verb.
