## Sense and ambiguity review

- COLUMN: regular opinion rubric in a newspaper; PILLAR: load-bearing upright.
- CHOICE: act of selecting; OPTION: available alternative before deciding.
- CITE: naming supporting source; QUOTE: repeating exact words.
- HINDER: difficulty progressing in a task; IMPEDE: physical obstacles slowing motion.
- AMBLE: slow easy gait; SAUNTER: pleasure-focused leisurely walk.
- ENGINE: fuel burning drives pistons; MOTOR: electrical current produces rotation.
- RAVEN/CROW: large wedge-tailed vs familiar fan-tailed corvid.
- SUBSIDE/ABATE/WANE: settling down vs reduced intensity vs gradual loss of strength.
- ANNUL/REPEAL/REVOKE: transaction vs law vs granted permission.
- LIGHT remains illumination; WATCH a wrist timepiece; NEST an arranging verb.

Closely related words such as SUBSIDE/ABATE and GIST/ESSENTIAL meaning inevitably
share concepts; grammatical framing and crossings resolve them without tricks.
Definitions do not claim that all synonym ambiguity can be eliminated.
