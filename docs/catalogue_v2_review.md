# Catalogue v2 content review

240 additions; all 60 v1 solution/clue pairs and metadata are retained unchanged. IDs use the existing lowercase-solution rule, never list indices. Difficulty is an editorial beginner-relative judgment, not a certified CEFR classification.

## Sense and duplication review

- CLOCK — Duvar saati and WATCH — Kol saati distinguish the two timepieces; MONTH — Takvim ayı distinguishes the existing MOON — Ay.
- FLOOR — Zemin vs SOIL — Toprak; BANK — Banka vs COAST — Deniz kıyısı; MARKET — Pazar yeri vs SHOP — Mağaza choose specific senses.
- SPRING means İlkbahar, BRANCH means Ağaç dalı, NEEDLE means Dikiş iğnesi, PEPPER means Biber, and LEAVE means Ayrılmak.
- BORROW — Ödünç almak and LEND — Ödünç vermek explicitly distinguish direction. CAMERA — Fotoğraf makinesi chooses the everyday photographic sense.
- UNCLE and AUNT retain both natural Turkish kinship possibilities. Clues remain translation-based; synonyms outside this bank can still exist.
- Familiar Turkish loanwords (Panda, Zebra, Robot, Pilot, Tenis, Otel) are retained where they are the normal everyday Turkish names.
- No regular-plural, tense, -ing, or US/UK spelling doublets were added. DEER is the dictionary base form; FRIDGE is established everyday vocabulary. Related RAIN/RAINBOW remain distinct concepts.
- The historical MOUSE — Fare metadata tag `home` is preserved, although `animal` would be a more direct category. No original clue error requiring correction was identified.
- Hard entries are common but less elementary concepts/actions relative to this beginner catalogue; not rare vocabulary inserted for crossings.
