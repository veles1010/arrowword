# French clue review

## Scope and method

900/900 canonical IDs, in the frozen English pack order: 300 Easy, 300 Medium, 300 Hard. All entries were authored independently from the canonical English sense and reviewed against answer/track, with Turkish available as a secondary sense check. No translation service or chained language translation was used. Turkish and English packs are unchanged.

The editorial pass covered all 900 entries: sense and part of speech, natural native wording, international readability, own-answer leakage, ambiguity, difficulty and mobile length. Concrete Easy definitions stay direct; Medium descriptions require a semantic step; Hard keeps distinctions rather than making clues cryptic.

## Reviewed root warnings

2 before and after review: GRAPE → «grappes» means bunches, not the English answer; PLATE → «plat» means flat, describing the vessel rather than naming it. These are native words with incidental prefix similarity, intentionally retained.

## Ambiguity and sense checks

COLUMN is a newspaper opinion feature, not a pillar. CHOICE is selection as an act; OPTION is an available alternative. ENGINE burns fuel to drive pistons; MOTOR converts electricity into rotation. CITE names evidence; QUOTE repeats exact words. INFER derives an unstated conclusion; DEDUCE proceeds from general principles. AMBLE is slow easy walking; SAUNTER adds leisurely pleasure. Legal withdrawal verbs retain their separate object (permission, decision, law, transaction). NEST remains a verb for placing objects inside one another; STEM means arise from a source.

Close synonyms still require crossings, as in the approved source game; they are not given identical definitions. Native cognates are permissible, but no own English answer is used as a clue token. Remaining playtest focus: brief nominal definitions and longest German-style compounds where applicable, rather than obscure trivia.

## Runtime and compatibility

This pack is presentation-only. Validation compares all IDs/order and the 108-board regression resolves all six packs without changing any board. Native speaker/device playtesting is still recommended before publication; automated integrity checks are not a substitute for that final product review.
